## Why this episode, and why now

The published episode ends on **"same hand. same hand as the book."** -- a connection, unresolved.
The paid chapter answers a *different* question (the mark matches her atlas; she puts the charm away).
So the public serial still has its hook intact and nothing to spoil.

The reach problem is the design constraint: the live episode reached **9 people**, the best Reel 40.
At that size, an episode that depends on remembering episode 1 cannot work. This one is built so a
cold viewer understands it in one slide -- **something was pushed under her door** -- while a returning
viewer gets the payoff of "the same hand" escalating from an object she owns to someone outside it.

**Serial hook strengthened:** episode 1 was a discovery (passive -- she found a thing).
Episode 2 is an intrusion (active -- someone else acted). That is the escalation the serial needs.

## Hard production rule: no text inside any image

The envelope carries **one hand-drawn star mark in ink -- no letters, no words, no address, no stamp.**
This is deliberate: generated handwriting comes out as gibberish and would break the frame. The
"same hand" idea is carried by the overlay copy, not by writing in the picture. The mark is the same
starburst already established on the charm, so it reads as canon rather than a new symbol.
