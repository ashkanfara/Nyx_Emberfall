## CTA: COMMENT MOON

The moon is Nyx's own mark: it's what she draws on her reply in Return Mail, and what comes back copied in
Three Stars. So "comment MOON" means the viewer signs with her mark. Every endcard says it, and the caption
explains what it does. **No promise is made that isn't kept**: no "comment for a link" and no promised DMs,
because there's no DM automation in this project.

Two risks the founder should accept knowingly:
- **Meta's engagement-bait rules.** Meta demotes posts that ask for comments as a bare instruction
  ("comment YES"). A CTA tied to the story is safer, but not immune. If Two Stars underperforms the
  account's average after 48 hours, drop the CTA line from the captions and keep only the endcard.
- **The repo's own voice rules.** They ban "comment below" in Nyx's mouth. COMMENT MOON is a different
  thing, an on-screen card, which is why it stays out of the voiceover.

| Reel | CTA line (the same on Instagram and TikTok) |
|---|---|
| Two Stars | if you'd have opened it too, comment MOON. |
| Above My Window | comment MOON if you'd leave it in the window. |
| Shadow Under Door | comment MOON if you'd have stayed by the door too. |
| Return Mail | comment MOON so I know someone's waiting with me. |
| Three Stars | **comment MOON if I should open it. if the moons win, I open it next.** |

**Three Stars is a real fork.** Like the s1e02 vote, the result has to be honoured. Decision rule, to be
written into the next episode's lock before it is planned: count MOON comments against comments that say
"wait" or "don't" over the first 72 hours. If MOON is ahead, or nobody answers, she opens it. If "wait" is
ahead, the next episode is about waiting.

## Fanvue companion outline

The rules come from prompts.py and the s1e02 companion:
- deeper, not the same slides behind a paywall;
- tease-first, never explicit;
- **never resolves a public open loop** (no sender, no meaning for the starburst, the ribbon or the key,
  and the third envelope stays sealed);
- 9:16 frames from the same masters and reference package;
- caption carries `#AIgenerated`, because Fanvue has no native label.

Each chapter is 3-4 new 9:16 frames plus a short first-person caption. **No companion images exist yet.**
Each one needs its own DRAFT lock and founder approval before generation (same route as
`episodes/s1e02_public/fanvue_companion/`).

## Final preflight checklist

Run it once per reel, per platform, before pressing Schedule. **Any FAIL stops that post.** Record evidence
(screenshot or link) for each tick.

### 0. Once, before anything else
- [ ] **Avoid a duplicate Two Stars.** Open the Metricool calendar for Sat 10 Oct. If Two Stars is already
      scheduled there for Instagram, either keep it and skip the Meta Business Suite post, or delete the
      Metricool post first. **Never both.** Screenshot the result.
- [ ] Check the Meta Business Suite planner and TikTok Studio's scheduled list for any existing Nyx post
      between 10 and 18 Oct.
- [ ] Confirm the five files are the approved `_cta_v1` versions (file name + date), not an earlier render.

### 1. File
- [ ] Vertical 9:16 at 1080x1920, and it plays start to finish locally.
- [ ] Length matches the approved cut (note it): reels 18-25 s, plus the endcard.
- [ ] Nothing important falls in the Instagram/TikTok UI zones (top ~250 px, bottom ~340 px, right-hand
      button column). That covers the subtitles, her face and the key prop.

### 2. Subtitle / voiceover / visual sync (the mismatch guard)
Watch with sound, then again muted.
- [ ] **Every spoken word is subtitled, word for word:** no missing, extra or reworded lines. Compare against
      the reel's approved script. If the reel's script differs from `reel/voiceover_script.json`, the reel's
      own approved script wins; note the difference.
- [ ] **Each subtitle chunk appears while those words are spoken.** Off by more than about 0.3 s = FAIL.
- [ ] **Each object is on screen when she names it:** the two-star envelope, the star anise, the chart
      corner and the moon, the light under the door and the shadows, the dark-blue envelope and the silver
      moon, the three-star envelope with the copied moon. Named before it appears, or after it's gone =
      FAIL.
- [ ] **Each reaction line plays over her face.** A cause line never plays after its effect shot.
- [ ] **The voice is the approved voice**, never the free TEST voice. Any frame carrying "TEST - generic voice
      - not for release" = FAIL.
- [ ] Nothing contradicts the earlier reels:
  - same Nyx, both fox ears, one tail, dark fur;
  - same wardrobe, black cat, moonlit window, low table, black ribbon and key, door;
  - the prop states follow the ledger (`<package>/PACKAGE.md`): for example, the third envelope is still
    sealed at the end of Three Stars.
- [ ] No legible text inside the art. The only text is the subtitles and the COMMENT MOON endcard.
- [ ] The endcard says COMMENT MOON exactly as approved, and the caption CTA line uses the same word.
- [ ] Music (if any) is licensed, sits under the voice and never masks a word.

### 3. Platform settings at schedule time
- [ ] **Instagram:** the "AI info" / AI label is switched on.
- [ ] **TikTok:** the "AI-generated content" disclosure is switched on.
- [ ] The caption is pasted from this file for **that** reel. Check the reel title against the caption's
      first line.
- [ ] Cover frame is a clean story frame (no subtitle caught mid-word); TikTok cover text if used: under 6
      words.
- [ ] Date and time read in Melbourne time (AEDT) in the scheduler. Check the time zone label, not just the
      clock.
- [ ] Instagram: "Also share to Facebook" set to whatever the founder decides (default off); no paid boost.
      TikTok: comments on, duet/stitch per account default; no promote.
- [ ] Nothing posts to X or Threads.

### 4. After each post goes live
- [ ] Open the live post on a phone: correct reel, caption, subtitles legible, AI label visible.
- [ ] Record the link, platform and time in the release log (`confirm-published` once the runner tracks
      this batch).
- [ ] Two Stars only: after 48 hours, check reach against the account average and decide whether the CTA
      line stays (see the CTA risks above).
- [ ] Three Stars only: count MOON against "wait" over 72 hours and record the result before the next
      episode is planned.
